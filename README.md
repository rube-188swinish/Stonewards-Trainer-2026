## 🎮 About the Game

Stonewards is a first-person action-adventure roguelite defense game developed and published by Banana Tiger Studio. It entered Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026, and supports solo play and online co-op for one to four players. Players control battle-ready dwarves who mine procedural caves, gather resources, forge upgrades, and defend dwarven strongholds from monster waves.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Stonewards**. This trainer enhances the core dig, forge, and defend gameplay loop. It can protect the player and King during defensive waves, simplify mining and resource management, provide ammunition and crafting advantages, and reduce the difficulty of roguelite progression during solo runs.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Stonewards, test builds, gather resources more freely, practice defensive encounters, or reduce the difficulty of solo roguelite runs.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Player Health** — Prevents the controlled dwarf from taking fatal damage during exploration and stronghold defense.
- **Unlimited Resources** — Provides a large or unlimited supply of mined materials used for crafting and upgrades.
- **Freeze Wave Timer** — Pauses the mission timer during mining and preparation phases, allowing more time to gather resources and forge upgrades.

### 📌 Mining

- **Resource Multiplier** — Increases the amount of valuable ore and materials collected from procedural mines.
- **Instant Mining** — Speeds up pickaxe mining so tunnels and resource deposits can be cleared quickly.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Prevents crafted ammunition from being consumed while defending the bastion.
- **One-Hit Kills** — Allows attacks to defeat regular monsters with a single successful hit.

### 📌 Crafting

- **Unlimited Forge Materials** — Allows ammunition, repairs, and available forge upgrades to be prepared without resource limits.

### 📌 Defense

- **Free Repairs** — Removes the resource cost of repairing damaged walls and defensive structures.
- **Protect the King** — Prevents the King from losing health, helping keep the mission active during monster assaults.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts dwarf movement speed for faster travel between the mines, forge stations, and defensive positions.

### 📌 Progression

- **Run Protection** — Helps preserve the player's run by preventing common combat and resource-related setbacks during solo play.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use standard first-person movement to travel through the bastion and procedural caves. The movement speed option is useful when returning from a mine before the next attack or repositioning along the stronghold walls. Disable it before entering menus or joining online sessions.

### Tools & Inventory

Mined ore and other materials support crafting, repairs, ammunition, and randomized forge upgrades. Use the resource and ammunition options before visiting the forge when you want to prepare freely, then disable them if you want to retain a more balanced progression experience.

### World & Environment

Each mission sends the dwarf crew from the base camp to a threatened dwarven stronghold. Between monster waves, enter the procedurally generated mines, collect valuable materials, explore branching tunnels, and return to the surface before the next assault. The timer option can provide additional preparation time.

### Combat & Weapons

Monster waves attack the bastion and attempt to breach its defenses while the King remains protected. Use unlimited health and King protection for safe practice, unlimited ammunition for extended defense, or one-hit kills to clear ordinary enemies quickly. Bosses and special encounters may require different trainer settings depending on the current Early Access build.
